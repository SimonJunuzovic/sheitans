Writing about Sheitans? Here is what you need.

In one line

Sheitans is a playful moral support app that helps people tame their inner demons, the habits they want less of.

A little longer

Most habit apps feel like a doctor's waiting room or a strict coach. Sheitans turns each habit into a demon you can name, outsmart and tame, each one its own neon creature. The joke is always on the demon, never on the person. Sheitans is moral support, not therapy, and makes no promises about recovery.
